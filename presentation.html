The Usability Barrier

Two blockers exist: Hardware access and App usability. Hardware is solved. We solve the human usability gap.

Our Solution

A voice-native co-pilot layered on top of any banking app

Zero retraining required. The user speaks naturally; the AI illuminates the path and speaks aloud.

🎙️

1. Speaks Naturally

In casual Urdu or English:

"مجھے بجلی کا بل جمع کرنا ہے"
🧠

2. Understands Intent

Classifies spoken intent using conversational SLM/LLM without rigid keyword matching.

✨

3. Visual & Voice Guide

Highlights exact buttons with glowing pulse rings, pointing arrows, and speaks each step aloud.

Who it serves: Elderly citizens • Rural populations • First-time smartphone owners • Anyone excluded by app complexity.
