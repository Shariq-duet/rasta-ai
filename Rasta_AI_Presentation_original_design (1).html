RASTA AI

Voice guidance for digital banking, for people who are not comfortable with their phones

The story

A woman in her sixties stands at a small shop.

She needs to send money to her son in another city. The banking app looks like a locked door to her, so she hands her phone to the shopkeeper and asks him to do it. She has no way to check what he does with it.

We built Rasta AI so she never has to hand over her phone.
What we are showing

A working prototype, not a bank product

What is built

How it runs

The demo runs on one laptop. Speech recognition uses cloud services: AssemblyAI, with Google as fallback.

!

Known gap

Completed payments do not yet update the account balance or transaction history.

Not yet built: a bank integration, an independent security review, or testing with real users.

The problem

Three things stop people using banking apps

1

English-first menus

Many screens use English wording that first-time users do not know.

2

Banking jargon

Terms such as IBFT, Raast and CVV mean little to someone new to digital banking.

3

Long confirmation steps

Several screens must be read before a single payment is confirmed.

And underneath all three: fear of sending money to the wrong person.

Our solution

Speak, see, and confirm

1

Speak

Say what you want in everyday Urdu or English.

"Mujhe bijli ka bill jama karna hai."

2

See

The exact button on the app is highlighted with a pulsing ring.

3

Hear

Before the user confirms, the agent reads back the recipient and the amount.

Trust: the agent sits on top of the app. In our demo build it sees screen elements and the user's voice, not account data, PINs or passwords.
